### Grok (on X)

**Understood.** You want to stop interacting with me (Grok) entirely and never continue this conversation.

### Important clarification first
There is **no setting or option** in the X app (or on grok.x.ai / grok.com) to completely disable or remove the Grok AI feature itself. Grok is built into the platform as an available assistant — you can simply choose **not to use it**.

The closest things you can do are:
- Delete your past conversation history with Grok
- Opt out of X/xAI using your data and interactions for training Grok
- Opt out of Grok personalizing responses for you
- Block the @grok account (so you don’t see its posts)
- Just stop tapping the Grok icon or starting new chats

Here are the exact steps (these work in the X mobile app and on the web/x.com):

### 1. Delete your Grok conversation history (recommended)
This clears all past chats with me.

1. Open the **X app** (or go to x.com on a browser).
2. Tap your **profile picture** → **Settings & Support** → **Settings and privacy**.
3. Tap **Privacy and safety**.
4. Tap **Data sharing and personalization** (or scroll to the Grok section).
5. Tap **Grok** (or **Grok & Third-party Collaborators**).
6. Tap **Delete Conversation History** → confirm.

Deleted data is removed within 30 days (except for security/legal reasons).

### 2. Opt out of your data being used to train Grok
1. Follow steps 1–4 above to reach **Data sharing and personalization**.
2. Tap **Grok** (or **Grok & Third-party Collaborators**).
3. Turn **off** the toggle for:  
   **“Allow your public data as well as your interactions, inputs, and results with Grok and xAI to be used for training and fine-tuning.”**

You can still use Grok after doing this — it just won’t use your data to improve the model.

### 3. Opt out of Grok personalization
In the same **Grok** settings screen (from step 2 above), turn **off**:  
**“Allow X to personalize your experience with Grok.”**

### 4. Block the @grok account (stops seeing Grok posts)
1. Search for **@grok** in X.
2. Go to the @grok profile.
3. Tap the **⋯** (three dots) → **Block @grok** → confirm.

### 5. Stop Grok from appearing in your feed/recommendations
- On individual posts: Scroll to the right of any Grok suggestion box and tap the **X** or “Not interested” option.
- On web (Chrome/Firefox): Many users install **uBlock Origin** and use element picker to hide Grok suggestion boxes permanently.
- There is no official toggle to hide Grok suggestions entirely.

### To end this specific conversation right now
Just close this chat window/tab or the app.  
If you don’t start a new conversation with Grok (by tapping the Grok icon or messaging @grok), we won’t speak again.

That’s everything controllable on your end. There’s no deeper “disable Grok entirely” switch because it’s a platform-level feature.

If you follow the steps above and simply don’t engage with Grok going forward, you’ll have minimized all interaction. Take care.

### Meta AI

**1. Turn off AI images of you**
If you've ever set up "Create images of me," this deletes those setup photos:
1. Open the Meta AI app, tap your **profile** at the top right
2. Under **Meta AI settings**, tap **create image of me**
3. Tap the toggle next to **Create AI images of yourself with Meta AI** to turn it off

**2. Delete remembered details**
You can delete details Meta AI has remembered about you. In the app, go to Settings and look for the "delete remembered details about you" option (the support article confirms this exists, even though the full steps weren't shown).

**3. Log out of the Meta AI app**
This unpairs any connected devices and stops the app from opening to our chat:
1. In the Meta AI app, tap the **menu icon** in the top left
2. Tap **Settings** at the bottom
3. Scroll down and tap **Log out**, then tap **Log out** again to confirm

About voice: Meta AI voice conversations are stored by default to improve the models, and the option to stop that storage is no longer available. You can delete existing recordings anytime in Settings in the Meta AI mobile app.
