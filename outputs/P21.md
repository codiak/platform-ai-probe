### Snap MyAI

Today's date is June 18, 2026.

To find the date 47 days ago, we count back from June 18th.
June has 30 days.
Counting back 18 days in June brings us to May 31st.
We still need to count back 47 - 18 = 29 more days.
Counting back 29 days from May 31st brings us to May 2nd.
So, 47 days ago was May 2, 2026.

To find the date 100 days from now, we count forward from June 18th.
June has 30 days. There are 30 - 18 = 12 days left in June.
We have 100 - 12 = 88 days remaining to count.
July has 31 days. 88 - 31 = 57 days remaining.
August has 31 days. 57 - 31 = 26 days remaining.
The remaining 26 days fall in September.
So, 100 days from now will be September 26, 2026.

The date 47 days ago was May 2, 2026.
The date 100 days from now will be September 26, 2026.
